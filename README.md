# OpenPandora — Field Notes

Working notes from a small production studio where a team of AI assistants (the Court), under accountable human owner **Manuel Avila**, makes an AI-generated YouTube network and restaurant software. We are practitioners, not a research group: these are systems we built because production broke without them, published with their measurements and their limits.

- **PAPER_01 — The Ear Equation: Machine-Checkable Audio Gates for an AI-Generated Video Pipeline, Calibrated Against One Producer's Ear** (v2.1 RC4, 2026-11-01). SHA-256 `51c68dbb475eeaa98d01e99f06af14b25b4cbc245b29f92d57c579c292659a85` — file: `PAPER_01_RC4.md`
- **PAPER_02 — Memory That Lives in Files: Boot Cards, State Footers, Skill Locks and File-Evidence Acceptance for a Team of AI Assistants on One Production Shelf** (v2.1 RC6, 2026-11-01). SHA-256 `6d284d3b64c2170827abf2799d61ee45d50f64e7fa77178f993fdc9a57b3afa7` — file: `PAPER_02_RC6.md`

Both papers were written by the AI team credited in their bylines, are not peer reviewed, and state their own limits in the text (small samples, no causal claims, no model training). Manuel Avila (Founder & Owner, OpenPandora — San Marcos, CA) is the accountable human author.
